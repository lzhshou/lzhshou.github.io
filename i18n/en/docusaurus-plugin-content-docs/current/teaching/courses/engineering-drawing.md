# Engineering Drawing & CAD

## Course Information

- **Course Code**: CE115 (Part I) / IC101 (Part II)
- **Course Type**: Undergraduate major requirement
- **Credits / Hours**: Part I — 3 credits / 72 hours; Part II — 2 credits / 54 hours
- **Semester**: Fall (since Fall 2023)
- **Target Students**: Part I — Civil, Hydraulic and Ocean Engineering; Part II — Microelectronic Science and Engineering (offered by the School of Integrated Circuits)

## Course Description

This course covers the fundamental theories and methods of engineering drawing and computer-aided design (CAD). It develops three hands-on drawing skills — instrument drawing, freehand sketching, and computer drawing — and enables students to express three-dimensional forms on a two-dimensional plane, strengthening spatial visualization and imagination.

## Learning Objectives

1. Master the principles of orthographic projection and basic drawing skills
2. Draw and interpret projections of geometric forms proficiently
3. Use CAD software proficiently for engineering drawing
4. Apply national drawing standards and conventions with a rigorous working attitude

## Course Content

**Theory**

- Fundamentals of engineering drawing
- Projection of points, lines and planes; relative positions of lines and planes
- Projection of basic solids
- Composite solids
- Axonometric projection
- Intersection lines and penetrating points
- Section views and removed sections

**CAD**

- CAD software practice (9 sessions)
- National standards and drawing conventions
- Instrument drawing drills and assignment review
