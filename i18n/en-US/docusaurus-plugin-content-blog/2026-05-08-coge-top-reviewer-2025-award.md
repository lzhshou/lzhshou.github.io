The Elsevier journal *Computers and Geotechnics* has announced its list of top reviewers for 2025, and Dr. Zhengshou Lai is among the recipients of the **Certificate of Excellence in Reviewing** (Top Reviewer 2025 Award).

**Journal Information:**
- Journal: *Computers and Geotechnics*
- Publisher: Elsevier
- Field: Computational geomechanics and numerical methods
- Editors-in-Chief: Prof. John S. McCartney, Prof. Jidong Zhao
- Editors: Prof. Stavroula Kontoe, Prof. Ha Bui

*Computers and Geotechnics* is a leading international journal in computational geotechnical engineering, publishing research on finite element, discrete element, material point and other numerical methods applied to soil and rock engineering. The award is given annually by the editorial board to a selected group of reviewers in recognition of the quality, timeliness, and constructiveness of their reviews throughout the year.
